Privacy Policy

TallyHo is designed as a private, on-device budget planning tool. This version does not send your budget entries or local profile information to TallyHo servers.

Information stored on your device

Your local profile, planned income, bills, savings, debts, settings, and purchase-check history are stored on your device. You choose what to enter. TallyHo does not connect to bank accounts, initiate financial transactions, or move money.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. TallyHo receives only the information needed from StoreKit to determine whether a TallyHo Premium subscription is active. Apple handles payment information according to Apple’s policies.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

This version does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or data brokers.

Backups and deletion

If you export a backup, the file is created at your request and handled through the destination you select. You can delete a local profile and its budget data from Settings. You can also reset all budget data from Settings.

Device authentication

If you enable Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode access, authentication is performed by iOS. TallyHo does not receive or store your biometric data.

Children’s privacy

TallyHo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because this version does not send profile or budget information to TallyHo servers, no child profile information is collected by the developer.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. For privacy questions, email deshasierapps@gmail.com or visit the DeShasier Apps support page.
